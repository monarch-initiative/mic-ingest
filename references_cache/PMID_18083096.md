Calcium ions (Ca(2+)) impact nearly every aspect of cellular life. This review 
examines the principles of Ca(2+) signaling, from changes in protein 
conformations driven by Ca(2+) to the mechanisms that control Ca(2+) levels in 
the cytoplasm and organelles. Also discussed is the highly localized nature of 
Ca(2+)-mediated signal transduction and its specific roles in excitability, 
exocytosis, motility, apoptosis, and transcription.
